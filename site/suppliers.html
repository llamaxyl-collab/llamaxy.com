Working with Llamaxy Limited

How we buy

Simple, predictable orders from a UK business customer.

Orders

Regular orders in full cartons and pallets. We are happy to meet your minimum order value and to start with a smaller first order.

Payment

Pro-forma payment by bank transfer (EUR or GBP) before dispatch. Credit terms only if you offer them later.

Logistics

We can collect from your warehouse (EXW/FCA) with our own freight partners, or use your carrier. We handle UK import, customs and VAT under our EORI number.

Our commitments to brands and distributors

Why distributors can trust us with their brands.

UK end consumers only

Stock bought from you is sold only to consumers in the United Kingdom. We do not resell to other traders and do not ship back into your territory.

Pricing & brand rules

We respect recommended and minimum advertised prices and the brand owner's listing and image guidelines.

Compliance

UK product safety, labelling and UK importer requirements are met for every product we sell. Documentation is kept for every shipment.
