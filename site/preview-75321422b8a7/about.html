About us

Llamaxy Limited is an independent UK importer and retailer based in Cambridge, trading since 2021. We specialise in branded toys, games and collectibles.

We sell online to consumers across the United Kingdom, with stock held in UK fulfilment centres. We are VAT registered and hold an EORI number for importing goods from the European Union. See For suppliers for how we work with distributors.

We are always interested in building long-term relationships with authorised distributors, wholesalers and brand owners.
