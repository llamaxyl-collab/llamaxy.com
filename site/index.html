What we do

We buy from authorised sources and sell to end consumers in the UK.

Import & purchasing

We buy brand-new stock from authorised distributors in the EU and UK in carton and pallet quantities, pay by bank transfer and arrange collection, freight and UK customs clearance ourselves.

UK retail

We sell to consumers across the United Kingdom through established online retail channels, led by Amazon.co.uk with stock held in UK fulfilment centres for fast delivery and full customer service.

Brand-safe selling

Only new, original products with correct EAN barcodes. We follow manufacturer pricing and brand guidelines, never resell to other traders and comply with UK product safety rules.
