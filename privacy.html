Who we are

Llamaxy Limited, 122 Hawkins Road, Cambridge, CB4 2RD, United Kingdom. Contact: info@llamaxy.com.

This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. Your basket is kept only in your own browser. Card payments are taken by Stripe on its secure page; we never see your card details.

Our internal tools and Google data

We run a private, internal business tool used only by our own staff. With the account owner's consent it reads our own company Gmail mailboxes (Google OAuth) to find supplier invoices, delivery updates and replies to our business emails. This data is used only for running our own business, is stored on our own secured server, is never sold or shared with third parties, and is not used for advertising or to train AI models. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Customers

When you order on this website we receive your name, email, phone number and delivery address from the payment page. We use them only to deliver your order, send you order and tracking emails and handle returns; our UK fulfilment partner receives your name, address and phone number to deliver the parcel. Order records are kept for 6 years for tax purposes.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask what data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or complain to the ICO (ico.org.uk). Email us at info@llamaxy.com.
