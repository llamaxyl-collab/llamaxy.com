Our internal tools and Google data

We run a private, internal business tool used only by our own staff. This data is used only for running our own business, is stored on our own secured server, is never sold or shared with third parties, and is not used for advertising or to train AI models. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
